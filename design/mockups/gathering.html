Woodcutting (yews), auto-detected. One pill per tree, from your click until the tree falls. The faint notches inside are your chop attempts (one every 4 ticks). Top: a log and its XP each time you get one. Tree falls: the pill ends in a small stump. If you were slow to click the next tree, a white "+2" shows the ticks you lost. Blink when done: when a tree falls, the whole bar flashes mint twice. The metronome is different on every count: a single thin white outline, every tick. Use the ▶ preview buttons to compare. Right: logs in your inventory and roughly when it'll be full. That one is an estimate from your recent catch rate (gathering is random), hence the "~". So there's no finish flag here, unlike cooking.
