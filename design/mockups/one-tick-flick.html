Metronome
1-tick flicking. Every tick is a beat, so the ▲ carets are gone. You double-click prayer once per tick (off, then straight back on) and it never drains. Middle row: a small dot in the prayer's colour = a clean flick, sitting where in the tick you clicked. Hollow = your two clicks landed in different ticks. Empty slot with × = you skipped the tick. A long pill = you left prayer on, so it drained. Right side: where in the tick your clicks land over the last 40 ticks (left edge = tick start, right edge = next tick). A tight hump in the middle is good; clicks near the right edge are the ones that split. Then your clean-flick streak.
