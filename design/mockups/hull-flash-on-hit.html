Hull flash on hit (toggle). When an enemy's hitsplat lands on you, that enemy's outline flashes yellow, the same yellow as hits on the bar, then fades over about half a second. It fires on the hitsplat, never on the swing, so it only makes visible what already happened. A miss (blue 0) flashes faint grey. Other enemies stay unmarked. There's an "off at bosses" switch, on by default, to stay clear of the boss-mechanic rule.
