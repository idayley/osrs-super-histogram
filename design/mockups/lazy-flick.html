Metronome. Every tick, the bar's outline gives a faint blink. On the tick before your beat (your moment to click prayer on) it gives a bright one. "Click cue only" keeps just the bright one. Three rows, one job each. Top: what hit you. Enemy icon, damage number, and a line down at the tick it landed: yellow = it hit you, faint = blocked. Middle: your prayer. One pill per stretch of prayer, coloured by which prayer. Bottom: what you did. Sword = you attacked (+1 = a tick later than your weapon allowed). Fish = you ate, with what it cost your attacks: +3, +5 for a combo, or free if your weapon was already ready. Potions are gone: they never delay you. ▲ = your every-4 beat.
