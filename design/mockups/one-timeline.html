One timeline, three lenses

The same 14 seconds of fighting an Abyssal demon, drawn through three modes. Every lane sits in the same place in every mode, and nothing is ever left out. A mode only dims what it doesn't care about, adds its own helpers, and changes the readout on the right.

Lanes, top to bottom: hits on you & XP · what you're doing · prayer (with points used) · your inputs (attacks, eats, clicks) · ticks
