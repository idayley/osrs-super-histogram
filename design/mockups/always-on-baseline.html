The always-on baseline: what the bar shows when no special mode is detected. Same three rows. Top: what came to you (XP in each skill's colour, a hit on you in yellow). Middle: what your character is doing right now (a pill while an action or animation runs, a faint one while you walk or run). Bottom: your clicks on the tick ruler. Right side: one thing worth knowing at a glance. AFK swaps the right side for a logout countdown and flashes the bar mint before you'd be logged out.
New: run energy as a thin gauge under the activity name, always there (turns white under 20%), and a running pill shows how much the run cost. Login time sits faint in the far right corner of every mode.

Questing / general play

Walking is a faint pill, actions (talk, use item, teleport) are solid. XP from anything shows up as you'd expect. Right: XP this session and the last thing you got.

AFK (NMZ, splashing, fishing at Barbarian)

One long pill counts up since your last click. The right side counts down to the 5-minute idle logout, and the bar flashes mint at 4:30 so you click before you're kicked. Hits on you still show.

Minimal (all off but XP)

For people who want almost nothing: the tick ruler, the metronome if on, and XP drops.
