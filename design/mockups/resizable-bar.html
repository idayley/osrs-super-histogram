What changes with size

TallerEverything scales up: pills, numbers, icons, the readouts. Tick width follows height, so the shape stays the same. WiderMore ticks of history. Nothing gets stretched. Below ~56 px tallYour actions row hides first (swords, eats, +N). Hits and XP stay. Below ~40 px tallOne row: pills only, hits become small yellow notches on top of them. Narrower than ~720 pxThe right-side readout (verdict, ETA, totals) hides. Narrower than ~480 pxThe activity name on the left hides too, leaving just the timeline.
